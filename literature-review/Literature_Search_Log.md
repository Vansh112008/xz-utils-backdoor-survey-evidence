## 5. Inclusion Criteria

Sources may be included when they:

1. Address CVE-2024-3094 or the XZ Utils incident.
2. Examine open-source software supply-chain security.
3. Discuss malicious code injection, dependency compromise or package integrity.
4. Examine software provenance, reproducible builds or secure CI/CD pipelines.
5. Present relevant empirical, theoretical or technical findings.
6. Come from an identifiable academic, institutional or technically credible source.
7. Fall within the required publication period where the assignment specifies one.

## 6. Exclusion Criteria

Exclude or separately classify sources when they:

1. Have no meaningful connection to the research objectives.
2. Are duplicate publications or duplicate versions of the same work.
3. Lack sufficient bibliographic information for reliable identification.
4. Are unsupported opinion pieces without technical or evidentiary value.
5. Repeat information already covered by a more authoritative source without adding value.

Do not automatically classify a technical advisory, preprint, workshop paper or industry report as a peer-reviewed journal or conference article. Record the publication type accurately.
